## 23.4 hospital beds and 36.8 physicians per 10,000 population in the Kingdom

The total number of hospitals in the Kingdom reached 516, with Riyadh Region recording the highest number at 115 hospitals. The number of primary healthcare centers and medical complexes reached 5,779. In 2024, the number of physicians in the Kingdom stood at 129,772, representing an increase of 14.54% compared to 2023, with 55,159 Saudi doctors. However, the total number of dentists reached 33,751, of whom 19,598 were Saudis. The number of nurses reached 243,336, comprising 179,177 females. Additionally, there were 6,139 midwives, including 1,721 Saudis, with a ratio of 11.92 midwives per 1,000 live births. The total number of pharmacists reached 46,856, recording an increase of 27.3% compared to the previous year.
