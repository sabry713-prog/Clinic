## The Chief Executive Officer Message

nupco is the leading provider of medical procurement, warehousing, and distribution services for pharmaceuticals, medical devices, and supplies in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

Our mission is to elevate the performance of the healthcare sector by strategically improving healthcare facilities across all public hospitals and medical centers in the Kingdom, and by enhancing the efficiency of spending on pharmaceuticals, medical devices, and supplies.

To achieve the best possible healthcare for patients, in line with the aspirations of the wise government and the goals of Vision 2030, nupco empowers government healthcare entities to provide exceptional and cost-effective care for all. In collaboration with our hospitals and suppliers, we are advancing a new business model and a new approach to healthcare, all through the power of integrated technology and connectivity. nupco provides high-quality services to its partners, prioritizing their needs and helping them deliver best healthcare services.

Therefore, nupco is expanding its market share in the healthcare sector to ensure the provision of sustainable and optimal healthcare facilities for the Saudi community, contributing to the improvement of healthcare services at a governmental level.

## A Supply Chain of Value

nupco is the largest value-driven and centralized health care procurement, re-exporting, warehousing and distribution company for pharmaceuticals, medical equipment and supplies in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Founded in 2009 and fully–owned by Public Investment Fund, nupco aims to elevate the performance of the healthcare sector by strategically improving the healthcare facilities in all public hospitals and medical centers in the Kingdom. Backed by strong insights and a powerful global network, nupco empowers healthcare institutions to deliver exceptional, cost-effective care to everyone.

Together with our member hospitals and suppliers, nupco is advancing towards fueling a new business model and a new approach to healthcare through the power of integrated technology and connectivity. Through its focused and perceptive approach towards improving healthcare facilities at the governmental level, nupco offers quality services to its customers. It prioritizes the clients’ needs and assists them in providing the best healthcare services. nupco extends its due share in the healthcare market to ensure sustainable and optimum health facilities for the entire Saudi community.

## Proudly Contributing to
Saudi Vision 2030

Recognizing healthcare as one of the main focus areas of Saudi Vision 2030, nupco is at the core of the overall healthcare reform program.

It establishes nupco as a major governmental company for financial competency in medical procurement with a flexible policy for purchasing, storing and distributing.

![](https://www.nupco.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Vison-2030-Logo.png)

### nupco achieves the following objectives of Saudi Vision 2030:

- Improve the efficiency and effectiveness of the healthcare sector through the use of information technology and digital transformation.
- Increase the efficient utilization of available resources.
- Improve the infrastructure, facility management, and safety standards in healthcare facilities.
- Enhance the quality of life and healthcare service provided to hospital outpatients.
- Improve quality and safety principles as well as the skills of service providers.
- Support local content.
