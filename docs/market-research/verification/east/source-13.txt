# Yidu Tech Inc. Announces FY2026 Annual Results: Return to Profitability, Strong Revenue Growth, and Dividend Declaration
## Key Highlights for Investors
* **Turnaround to Profit:** Yidu Tech Inc. achieved a remarkable turnaround from a net loss of RMB135.2 million in FY2025 to a net profit of RMB78.8 million for the year ended 31 March 2026. This marks a significant milestone and a new chapter for the Group.
* **Revenue Growth:** Total revenue grew by 14.6% year-on-year to RMB819.3 million, driven by all three core business segments.
* **Dividend Declared:** The Board recommends a final dividend of HK\$0.04 per share for FY2026 (compared to nil for FY2025), subject to shareholder approval at the upcoming AGM.
* **Substantial Improvement in Cash Flow:** Net cash used in operating activities narrowed by 91.2% to RMB21.9 million, with positive net cash flow recorded in the second half of FY2026 (RMB29.3 million).
* **Strong Gross Margin Expansion:** Gross profit margin improved from 33.5% to 36.4%, reflecting efficiency gains and higher quality revenues.
* **Large-Scale Share Buyback:** The Company repurchased 50,015,000 shares on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange for approximately HK\$283.5 million, reflecting confidence in long-term value and aiming to enhance shareholder returns.

## Segment Performance and Business Review
### AI for Life Sciences (Life Sciences Solutions)
* Revenue increased 9.4% to RMB270.4 million; gross margin at 31.9%.
* Served 98 life sciences clients, including 17 of the top 20 multinational pharmaceutical companies.
* Completed or participated in 516 clinical research projects and 320 real-world research projects.

...

### AI for Care (Health Management Platform and Solutions)
* Revenue jumped 37.6% to RMB167.8 million, with gross margin at 36.4%.
* Yidu Tech remained the main platform operator for Shenzhen and Beijing Hui Min Bao, covering over 6 million and 19 million insured, respectively.
* Fast-claims service model improved claims processing efficiency by 60–70%.
* Innovative digital therapeutic solutions for chronic disease management (notably diabetes) rolled out in Hainan, Shanxi, and Tianjin.
* Active user base exceeded 22 million.

* **Balance Sheet:**
+ Total assets: RMB4.32 billion; net cash and equivalents: RMB2.08 billion at period-end.
+ No material acquisitions/disposals or capital commitments in the year.

# 盈康醫療科技（Yidu Tech Inc.）2026財政年度業績詳情（廣東話版）
## 投資者重點摘要
* **成功扭虧為盈：** 公司由2025財年虧損1.35億元人民幣，轉為2026財年盈利7,880萬元人民幣，標誌公司發展重要里程碑。
* **營收持續增長：** 總收入按年增長14.6%至8.19億元人民幣，三大核心業務均錄得增長。
* **首次派息：** 董事會建議2026財年派發每股HK\$0.04末期息（2025財年無派息），需股東大會通過，反映公司對現金流有信心。
* **現金流大幅改善：** 經營活動現金流出按年收窄91.2%，下半年錄得正現金流（2,930萬元人民幣）。
* **毛利率顯著提升：** 毛利率由33.5%升至36.4%，反映收入質素提升及成本效率增強。
