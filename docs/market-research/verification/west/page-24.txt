# How Mankato Clinic Boosted Both Clinician Productivity and Patient Experience with Nabla

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/67c6ddc58c9e7b3f7d095cb3/6a103356a50ebb103c0a7007_mankato_clinic_ltd_cover.webp)

Every clinician speciality that saw double-digit productivity gains also reported double-digit increases in patient satisfaction

Mankato Clinic, one of Minnesota’s largest independent physician-owned healthcare organizations, has been serving its community for more than a century with a strong commitment to patient-centered care. Like many healthcare organizations, Mankato clinicians were burdened by extensive documentation requirements, which limited patient interaction time and contributed to provider fatigue. The clinic sought a solution that could simultaneously improve clinician productivity and enhance the patient experience.

The study compared performance metrics for the twelve months before and after the adoption of Nabla, an advanced ambient AI technology used to support clinical documentation during patient encounters. Over 100 healthcare providers at Mankato Clinic in southern Minnesota used the technology more than 8,000 times to assist with visits.

Between May 2024 and May 2025, Nabla was used by 102 clinicians in 8,208 patient encounters. Nearly half of the clinicians were heavy users, with 48 providers using the ambient technology in at least 100 encounters each.

The clinicians using Nabla represented a wide range of ages, experience levels, and specialties. To be included in the study, clinicians had to meet two criteria: they must have used Nabla continuously for at least twelve months, and they must have been practicing at Mankato Clinic for at least twelve months prior to adoption.

## Clinician Productivity

Mankato Clinic saw an 8.15% year-over-year increase in Work Relative Value Units (wRVUs) across 23 specialties. The clinicians who experienced the strongest wRVU gains typically treated more complex cases or operated in documentation-heavy environments. Ten of the 23 specialties saw above-average productivity increases.

## Patient Experience

To evaluate how ambient AI impacted patients, surveys were administered following visits that used Nabla.

Mankato Clinic’s Net Promoter Score **increased by 7 points** during the first year of adoption, **rising from 87 to 94.**

To be included in the analysis, specialties had to receive at least 40 patient responses. Gastrointestinal saw the greatest improvement in patient satisfaction, with a 44% increase, followed by Cardiology with a 15% increase.

Taken together, these results demonstrate that Nabla’s advanced ambient AI technology can deliver measurable benefits at scale. By reducing administrative tasks, Nabla allowed Mankato clinicians to spend more time with patients. Clinicians experienced significant productivity gains, while patients reported higher satisfaction, often in the same specialties where efficiency improved the most.

For Mankato Clinic, Nabla proved to be a transformative technology, empowering clinicians, elevating patient care, and delivering measurable ROI within the first year of use. Notably, every specialty that saw double-digit productivity gains with Nabla also reported double-digit increases in patient satisfaction, demonstrating a direct link between clinical efficiency and the patient experience.

These findings provide evidence that AI-driven documentation support can enhance both provider efficiency and patient experience simultaneously. The results included measurable financial gains and a markedly improved patient experience.
